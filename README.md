## 📐 Dashboard Components

The Tableau dashboard includes:

- **KPI Table** - Total Sales, Total Profit, Avg Profit Margin, Order Count, Avg Order Value, Units Sold by category
- **Monthly Sales Trend (Dec'21-Dec'24)** - Line chart with MoM % change labels
- **Order Revenue Contribution** - Pie chart (Bulk vs Small orders)
- **Products by Total Revenue** - Horizontal bar chart ranked by profit (Monitor, Camera, Laptop top 3)
- **Regional Category Heatmap** - Sales values across 4 regions × 3 categories with color intensity
- **Month-over-Month Sales & Profit Comparison** - Bar chart with % change labels

**Top Products by Revenue**

**Monthly Trend Insight**

Sales peak in June 2023 (3.64% MoM growth) and dip in December 2023 (1.68% - lowest point). May shows the strongest MoM surge at +25.89%.
